So What privacy notice.

Draft for review; not yet effective. The support-record retention period and handling of separately submitted support information must be confirmed before this notice is published.

This notice describes the So What plugin published by Jason Claybrook, with support through Simple eSystem. It is specific to So What; other Simple eSystem website features and services have separate data practices.

Conversations in your AI account

So What supplies instructions and reference material to the AI product in which you use it. The current plugin has no publisher-operated server, external data connector, separate account, analytics endpoint, or payment flow. It does not separately transmit your conversation to Jason Claybrook or Simple eSystem.

The AI provider receives and processes what you enter. Its policies, account settings, and available controls govern conversation storage, use, sharing, and deletion. Using So What does not change those settings.

Information held by the publisher

Jason Claybrook and Simple eSystem do not separately collect or retain your So What conversations through this plugin. There is consequently no separate publisher-held conversation copy for us to sell, share, or delete.

If you contact support

You may choose to provide contact details, a description of a problem, or an excerpt. That is a separate interaction with Simple eSystem, outside the plugin. Do not send a full conversation or sensitive information unless it is necessary for your question.

Pending publisher confirmation: the purpose, service providers with access, retention period, and deletion procedure for support records will be stated here before publication. This draft makes no retention commitment.

Your choices

You can stop using or uninstall So What at any time. Use your AI provider's controls for conversation history and deletion. For questions about information you separately gave Simple eSystem, call 817-541-1750 and ask for So What privacy support.

Visiting this website

This plugin notice does not describe the hosting logs, cookies, or other features of simpleesystem.com. Those practices require their own website notice. The So What pages do not add a conversation form or a separate So What analytics script.
