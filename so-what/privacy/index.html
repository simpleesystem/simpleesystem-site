So What privacy notice.

Effective November 18, 2026

This notice describes the So What plugin published by Jason Claybrook and separately submitted So What support requests handled by Simple eSystem.

Conversations in your AI account

So What supplies instructions to the AI product in which you use it. The current plugin has no publisher-operated server, external data connector, separate account, analytics endpoint, or payment flow. It does not separately transmit your conversation to Jason Claybrook or Simple eSystem.

Your AI provider processes what you enter. Its policies, account settings, and available controls govern conversation storage, use, sharing, and deletion. Using So What does not change those settings. We do not hold a separate copy of your conversation through the plugin.

Information you choose to send for support

Contact us through help@simpleesystem.com or the Simple eSystem contact form. We receive the information you choose to submit, such as your name, contact details, description of the issue, and any examples, screenshots, or conversation excerpts you include. A conversation excerpt you send us becomes part of your support record, separate from the original conversation in your AI account.

We use support information to understand and respond to your request, coordinate related work, and learn from previous requests to improve our work and assistance.

Who handles support information

The Simple eSystem team handles requests using its project-management information and integrated AI tools. Team members and the providers of the communication, project-management, and AI services used in that process may receive or process support information as part of handling and learning from requests. Support information is therefore not confined to the So What plugin or to human-only review.

Retention

We retain resolved support requests indefinitely as project-management information and a source of learning from prior work. We do not automatically delete them after a fixed period. You may request deletion through the same support channels.

Deletion and other choices

To request deletion of information you separately submitted to us, contact help@simpleesystem.com or the Simple eSystem contact form and identify the request or information involved. Our team handles deletion requests through those channels. Do not send identity documents, passwords, or other secrets with the request.

For the conversation held by your AI provider, use that provider's history and deletion controls. We cannot delete a conversation from your AI account. You can stop using or uninstall So What at any time.

Share only what is needed

You do not need to send your full AI conversation for support. Remove unrelated personal or confidential business information from examples. Never send passwords, API keys, verification codes, payment-card details, government identifiers, or private medical records.

Visiting simpleesystem.com

The support practices above cover information you submit to us about So What. Other website features, hosting logs, cookies, and services may involve additional processing described in the Simple eSystem website privacy policy.
