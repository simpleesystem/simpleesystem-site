The service

So What is a conversational discovery and planning method published by Jason Claybrook. It helps users explore motivations, compare outcomes, and draft actions and timelines inside a supported AI product.

Price and availability

There is no separate fee for So What. Public availability is intended for all countries supported by the distribution platform. Your AI provider may require an account or paid plan and may impose its own access limits. Its terms also apply to your use of that product.

Your decisions and responsibilities

AI-generated responses can be mistaken or incomplete. Check important facts and review suggested estimates and plans before relying on them. A suggested owner, deadline, or approval is a proposal unless the person with authority has agreed to it.

So What offers general reflection and planning help. It does not replace professional medical, legal, financial, or other specialized advice when that is needed.

Actions outside the conversation

Writing a plan does not send messages, reserve services, authorize spending, make commitments, or schedule reminders. Any external action requires a capable tool and appropriate authorization.
