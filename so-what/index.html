Make the goal worth the effort.

So What helps you understand why a goal matters before you pour time into it. Explore your reasons, consider the outcomes, and decide what deserves a place on your calendar.

Created by Jason Claybrook for personal, family, community, and business decisions.

Public plugin access is being prepared. So What will be offered free in all supported countries. Your AI provider's account requirements, fees, and availability still apply.

Follow the reason. Choose the next step.

  1. Explore what matters. Answer one question at a time. After three answers, choose to keep exploring, follow another motivation, or map the outcomes. You can pause or move on earlier.
  2. Consider both paths. Look at what changes and what stays the same if you act or decide against it—including benefits, costs, and limits.
  3. Make a workable plan. Connect chosen outcomes to actions, owners, dependencies, completion evidence, and a realistic timeline.

Start with something real.

“I want more time with my family, but I don't know which part of my work to change first.”

“Help me plan a reunion around what matters most to the people coming.”

“I have a business idea. Help me decide whether building it would achieve the result I want.”

You set the direction.

A practical reason is enough. You do not need a painful story, a product to buy, or a reason to use AI. Keeping things as they are, trying a smaller experiment, or deciding against a project can all be useful outcomes.

Your conversation stays with your AI provider.

The So What plugin has no separate service that sends your conversation to Jason Claybrook or Simple eSystem. Your AI provider handles the conversation under its own policies. Read the So What privacy notice.

What to expect

So What guides a conversation and helps draft plans. It does not independently book appointments, send messages, spend money, or schedule reminders. Review suggested facts, estimates, and decisions before acting.
