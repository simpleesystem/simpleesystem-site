Consistent information

Name, address, phone, and hours must match.

A search engine trusts a fact more when it sees it the same way in many places. One listing with an old phone number weakens all the others.

  • Business name written one way everywhere.
  • Address and service area, matching your website.
  • One main phone number on every listing.
  • Current hours, including holidays.
  • Correct categories and a clear list of services.

We set the correct version once and apply it across every listing. We also find and fix old or duplicate entries.

Reviews and photos

Fresh photos and real reviews support every listing.

Customers choose between listings on photos and reviews. Listings with current photos and recent reviews look alive, and search engines treat them that way.

We help you add photos of your team, your work, and your location. Review requests and replies belong to reputation management, which works from the same listings.

Why AI cares

AI platforms use your listings as a source.

When ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity answers a question about a local business, it reads listings, directories, and reviews along with your website. If those disagree, the answer can be wrong.

We check what the platforms say about you in the AI visibility check, then correct the listings that feed the error. Listings also support the broader search visibility program.

Questions

Common questions about local listings

Which listings do you manage?

Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, Nextdoor, and relevant directories. We choose directories by your industry and area.

Why does Bing matter?

Yahoo's results come from Bing, so a strong Bing presence covers both. Some AI platforms also draw on Bing data.

What does consistent information mean?

Your business name, address, phone number, hours, and categories read the same on every listing and on your website. Differences confuse search engines and AI platforms.

Do listings affect what AI says about me?

Yes. AI platforms read listings and directories as sources. Correct, matching listings make a correct answer more likely.
