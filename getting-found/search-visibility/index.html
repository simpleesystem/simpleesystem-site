Three terms, plainly

SEO, AEO, and GEO each reach a different kind of search.

Customers use all three, often in the same week.

SEO

Search engine optimization. It helps search engines such as Google and Bing rank your pages in the list of results.

AEO

Answer engine optimization. It helps answer engines, including the AI answer at the top of Google, quote your page as the direct answer to a question.

GEO

Generative engine optimization. It helps generative AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Grok recommend and describe your business correctly.

All three depend on the same base: clear pages, accurate business information, and content that answers real questions. See also local listings, which feed all three.

Questions

Common questions about search visibility

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO ranks your pages in search results. AEO gets your page quoted as the direct answer. GEO gets your business recommended and described correctly by AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Gemini.

How long does it take to see results?

The first 30 days produce a baseline. By day 90 you have new content live and a first AI visibility comparison. CJW Chiropractic saw AI citations about six weeks after launch.

Who writes the content?

We do, using a method that captures your voice and expertise. You review everything, and nothing publishes without your approval.

Do I need a new website first?

Not always. If your current site is fast, clear, and structured well, we work on it as it stands. If it is not, we fix the foundation first. About a third of our Fred deployments needed website and content work before anything else.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We ask five platforms the questions your customers ask, record every answer, and count how often you are cited. We compare that count with local competitors each month.
