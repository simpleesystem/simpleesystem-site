What changed

Customers now get one answer instead of ten links.

A search used to return a list of links. Now ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Grok return a recommendation, and Google puts an AI answer above the list.

People trust the first good answer. If your business is not in it, or the answer gets your hours, services, or location wrong, the customer moves on and you never know they looked.

Getting found now covers three things. Search engines have to rank you. Answer engines have to quote you. Generative AI platforms have to describe you correctly. We work on all three together.
