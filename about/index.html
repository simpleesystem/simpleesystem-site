How we work

We start with the foundation.

Of roughly 30 Fred deployments, about a third needed foundational website and content work before the assistant could do its job.

An AI assistant can only answer from what your site says. A slow site, thin pages, or inconsistent business information limits every other effort. So we check the foundation first and fix it, then build getting found and engagement on top of it.

The work runs in 90-day cycles. Each cycle starts with real customer questions, turns the gaps into content in your voice, and measures the result. Nothing publishes without your approval. You get a plain-language report every month.

We run three pillars together: getting found, your website, and engagement. We don't run paid ads. Leads come from the three working together.

Security posture

Each site runs in its own isolated space, behind a global security network that handles attack protection and encryption. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email, never a password alone. We run nightly file checks and nightly backups, apply updates weekly, and ship security fixes by the next business day. If a site goes down, we respond the same day.
