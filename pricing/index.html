Terms

How billing works.

Half to start, half at go-live

Website builds are paid 50% to start and 50% when the site goes live. We both carry risk, and we're both motivated to finish. The site is the anchor for everything else we do, so getting it live comes first.

180 days to prove it

Every engagement starts with a 180-day commitment. That is two full 90-day cycles. After that, you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

More than one location

Some businesses run several locations on one site. Others need a site for each. The setup changes the work, so we price multiple locations when we scope your plan.

No paid ads

We don't run ads or take a cut of ad spend. The money goes into your content, your site, and the systems that answer your customers.

Questions

Pricing questions

How much does search visibility cost?

Search visibility starts at $2,500 a month, plus a one-time setup of $2,000. It covers SEO, AEO, GEO, local listings, monthly AI citation tracking, and a monthly report.

Is AI visibility priced separately?

No. AI visibility tracking and content for AI platforms are part of search visibility. The short AI visibility check is free.

How much does a new website cost?

Website builds are a one-time fee starting at $4,000. The final price depends on the number of pages and features, and we confirm it in writing after a short call. You pay 50% to start and 50% at go-live. Hosting and security is $199 a month per site.

Why do you split the build payment?

Half up front and half at go-live means we both carry risk and we're both motivated to launch. The website is the anchor for search visibility and Fred, so a live site matters to both of us.

How much does Fred cost?

An AI assistant starts at $297 a month per site, plus a one-time setup of $2,000 that includes testing before launch. Automated follow-up is a separate $497 a month.

How much does automated follow-up cost?

Automated follow-up is $497 a month. It sends the right email or text after every form fill, call, quote, booking, and Fred conversation, and routes ready leads to your team. It also gives you one contact list that tracks every lead and connects to the tools you already use.

Is there a contract?

Every engagement starts with a 180-day commitment. After that, you can cancel any service with 30 days' notice.

How do you price more than one location?

It depends on whether your locations share one website or each have their own. We price multiple locations when we scope your plan. Call 817-541-1750 or talk to Fred to start.
