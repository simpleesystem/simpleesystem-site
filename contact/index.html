Before you reach out

What happens on a first call?

We ask about your business, your customers, and what you have in place today. Then we tell you where we'd start and whether we're a fit. If it makes sense, we follow up with a written plan and price.

What's the difference between a call and a demo?

A call is a conversation about your business and what to fix first. A demo shows the work: a live client site, Fred answering and scoring a lead, and your own AI visibility snapshot.

How fast will I hear back from a message?

We reply within one business day. Fred answers right away at any hour and alerts our team when a person is needed.

Is there a commitment to start?

Calls, demos, and the AI visibility check are free. If you become a client, the engagement starts with 180 days, then 30 days' notice to cancel. See pricing.
