Why migrate

Get the platform without rebuilding the site.

A rebuild is not the only way onto a better platform. If your site works, we keep it and change where it lives.

How a migration runs

Six steps, and nothing goes live untested.

StepWhat happens
1. InventoryWe list every page, file, form, and feature on your current site, so nothing gets left behind.
2. CopyWe copy the site to our platform. Your live site stays untouched and keeps serving visitors.
3. Test on a private addressWe check pages, forms, images, and speed on a private address that only your team and ours can see.
4. Map redirectsEvery old address that changes is pointed to its new one, so rankings and bookmarks carry over.
5. CutoverWe switch your domain to the new site at a time we agree with you, after you approve the test copy.
6. Watch after launchWe monitor the site, check the redirects, and fix anything that turns up.

Questions

Common questions about website migrations

Will my site go down during the move?

No. Your current site keeps serving visitors while we copy and test. The switch happens at cutover, at a time we agree with you.

Do I have to redesign my site?

No. We move the site as it stands. If you want a redesign, we can do it after the move or instead of it. See website builds.

Will I keep my search rankings?

That is the goal of the redirect mapping. Every old address that changes points to its new one. We watch the site after launch to catch anything we missed.

Which platforms can you move?

WordPress, Drupal, and static sites. If your site runs on something else, call 817-541-1750 and we will tell you whether it can move.

How long does a migration take?

It depends on the size of the site and what it does. We give you a schedule after the inventory step. Most migrations take one to two weeks.

What does a migration cost?

Migrations are included with hosting. Hosting is $199 a month per site. See pricing. Every engagement starts with a 180-day commitment, then 30 days' notice to cancel.
