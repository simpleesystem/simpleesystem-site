Partners

You keep the client. We deliver under your name.

For agencies, consultancies, and firms that want to offer getting found, websites, and AI assistants without hiring a team to build and run them.

The short answerPartners sell our services as their own, and we deliver and operate them under the partner's name. You own the relationship and earn recurring revenue on monthly services.

Apply to partner Talk to Fred
What partners get

A full service line without building one.

Recurring revenue

Getting found, hosting and security, follow-up, and AI assistants are monthly services. Each client you bring adds revenue that repeats.

No developers or servers

We build the sites, host them, secure them, and run the assistants. You don't hire developers, manage servers, or carry the on-call burden.

A separate identity layer

Each partner and each client gets separate access, so no one shares platform keys. A problem in one account can't reach another.

Our clients include Bon Air Service Company, CJW Chiropractic Solutions, and the City of Grand Prairie Parks. See our work

How partner pricing works.

Partner pricing is a discount off list price, and the discount varies by service. Terms are available on request.

You set what your client pays. List prices are on our pricing page. Ask for the partner sheet when you apply. Partner engagements follow the same terms as direct ones: a 180-day commitment, then 30 days' notice to cancel.

You decide. We deliver under your name, and your clients deal with you.

No. You keep the client. We work through you unless you ask us to join a call.

Marketing agencies, web agencies, consultancies, and firms that serve local businesses, nonprofits, or public agencies.
