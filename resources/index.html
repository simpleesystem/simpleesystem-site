Assessment

AI readiness assessment.

A short assessment that shows where your business is ready for AI and where it isn't.

It covers five areas.

  • Sales process. How leads reach you and what happens next.
  • Data. What you know about your customers and where it lives.
  • Technology. The tools and website you run today.
  • Team. Who would use and own the change.
  • Business readiness. Goals, budget, and timing.

You get a plain-English summary with the first steps we'd take.
