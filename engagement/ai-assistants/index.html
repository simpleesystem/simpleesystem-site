What Fred does on your site

Fred is the assistant we know best, and he is the one on this site. Each client's Fred is trained on that client's own content.

  • Answers from your content. He uses what you have published and approved. He does not guess.
  • Stays in his role. The limits are part of how he is built, not instructions he can ignore.
  • Hands off to people. When a question needs a person, he passes it to your team.
  • Books appointments. Customers book in the conversation, at any hour.
  • Scores every lead. Your team calls the best leads first.

Testing

Tested before launch and every month

Before launch and every month after, each assistant faces hundreds of hard, persistent customer questions.

Every gap the testing finds becomes new content on your site. Fred gets better each month, and so does your website. Nothing new publishes without your approval.

Lead score

Every conversation gets a score

Fred scores each conversation on intent, trust, capacity, authority, and timeline. Hot leads are routed to your team right away.

Your team spends its time on the people who are likelier to buy. Leads who are not ready yet go into automated follow-up.

Questions

AI assistant questions

What is an AI assistant?

An AI assistant is an AI team member on your site. Fred answers customer questions from your content, books appointments, scores leads, and hands off to your people.

Can Fred say something wrong or off-topic?

He answers only from your content and is built so he cannot step outside his role. Each assistant is tested with hundreds of hard questions before launch and every month, and every gap becomes new content.

What happens when Fred can't answer?

He hands the conversation to a person on your team. The gap is also added to your content plan so the next customer gets an answer.

Does Fred work for medical or chiropractic practices?

Yes, as a front desk. He handles hours, services, insurance questions, and booking. He never discusses symptoms or medical history.

What is the difference between Simple eSystem and Fred?

Fred is the AI team member. Simple eSystem is the service that deploys him, tests him, and keeps your content current. Product details are at talktofred.ai.

Can you build a custom assistant?

Custom assistants, apps, and automation are handled by our parent company, Simple AI System.
