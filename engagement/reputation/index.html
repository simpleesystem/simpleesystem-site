Happy customers come back and say so in public.

Reviews shape who customers call and who AI platforms recommend. We ask for them at the right moment, watch for new ones, and answer every one.

What we do

Request, monitor, and respond

Reviews requested at the right moment

A request goes out soon after a job is done or an appointment ends, when the customer is happiest. It arrives by text or email with a direct link.

Reviews monitored

New reviews are watched across the platforms that matter to your customers. Your team sees them quickly and does not have to check each site.

Reviews answered

Each review gets a reply. Thanks for the good ones. A calm, specific response for the hard ones, with a way to make it right.

The research

Stars affect revenue

A Harvard Business School working paper by Michael Luca studied Yelp ratings and restaurant sales. Each added star lifted revenue 5 to 9 percent for independent restaurants.

The study covers independent restaurants, so treat it as evidence that ratings move real money, not as a promise for your trade. It is also why a steady flow of honest reviews is worth building.

Timing

Why the moment matters

A customer is happiest right after the problem is solved. That is when a request gets answered.

Asking a week later, or never, leaves reviews to the customers who are upset enough to post on their own. A timely request gives your happy customers a voice too. The request is triggered by the job or visit, so your staff does not have to remember it.

Search and AI

Reviews feed local search and AI answers

Google weighs review count, rating, and freshness for local results. AI platforms read the same public signals when they decide whom to recommend.

  • Recent reviews show a business that is active today.
  • Reviews that name your services and city help platforms match you to real questions.
  • Replies show that a person is paying attention.

Reviews work best alongside strong local listings and a site built to answer questions. See search visibility for how they connect.

Questions

Reputation questions

How do you get more reviews?

We send a review request at the right moment, soon after a job or visit, with a direct link. Making it easy and timely is what raises the number of reviews.

Do you write fake reviews?

No. Every review comes from a real customer. We ask, we make it easy, and we never write or buy reviews.

What happens with a bad review?

We flag it to your team quickly and help draft a calm, specific reply. A good response shows future customers how you handle problems.

Do reviews affect AI recommendations?

Reviews are public signals that AI platforms and local search can read. Fresh, detailed reviews help them see that your business is active and trusted.

What did the Harvard study find?

A Harvard Business School working paper by Michael Luca found that each added Yelp star lifted independent restaurant revenue 5 to 9 percent.
